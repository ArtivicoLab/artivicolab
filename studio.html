Studio.

A hundred generated mixes, for the lab only

Not linked from anywhere and marked noindex. A generator builds one hundred loops from the synth's instruments: a drum style, a bass, a chord voice, a lead, a key, a tempo and a two-bar hook, all from a fixed seed. No two share a line-up, and Mix 37 is always the same Mix 37. Use it to find the next sound worth finishing by hand. Any mix can be played in the footer and downloaded as WAV.
