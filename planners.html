How the planners use your Google account

Connecting Google is optional. When you choose to connect, a planner requests only the Google Drive “drive.file” permission , access limited to the single spreadsheet the app creates for you, and nothing else in your Drive. Your sign-in stays in your browser and talks to Google directly from your device. ArtivicoLab operates no server and never receives, stores, or sells your data.
